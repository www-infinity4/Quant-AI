A knowledge center and AI assistant for buyers and sellers. Bring a quant in, and the AI reads its packet and receipts to help you stock and shop smarter.

How quants work here

  • Every quant ships with a data packet. Bring one in to add it to your wallet.
  • Each quant gives you exactly one search, so more quants means more insight.
  • Searches return unified wallet addresses that shop or like an item, never anyone's history.
  • Quants can be spent between users, so you may hold quants you did not create.
